# 🧭 Core difference

- **frem** = direction / movement (“forward”)
- **fremme** = state / result (“arrived / present at a place”)

---

# 📍 1. **frem** = forward (movement or direction)

You use **frem** when something is _moving forward_ or going ahead.

### Examples:

- Gå **frem**! = Go forward!
- Han gikk **frem** til døren = He went forward to the door
- Vi må se **fremover** = We must look forward

👉 Think: **action / movement**

---

# 📍 2. **fremme** = arrived / present

You use **fremme** when you are **already there** (the result of travel).

### Examples:

- Vi er **fremme i Oslo** = We have arrived in Oslo
- Toget er **fremme** = The train has arrived
- Er dere **fremme** nå? = Are you there now?

👉 Think: **state / result**

---

# ⚖️ Simple comparison

| Word   | Meaning         | Type     |
| ------ | --------------- | -------- |
| frem   | forward         | movement |
| fremme | arrived / there | result   |

---

# 🔥 Easy memory trick

- **frem = “go forward” (verb direction)**
- **fremme = “we made it!” (arrival state)**

---

# 🚗 Real-life situation

### While traveling:

- Nå går vi **frem** til toget
- Vi er snart **fremme**

👉 First movement → then arrival

---

# ⚠️ Common mistake

### ❌ Vi er frem Oslo

### ❌ Vi er fremme Oslo

### ✅ Vi er fremme **i Oslo**

---

# 🧠 Key sentence pair

- Vi kom **frem** til Oslo (movement)
- Vi er **fremme i Oslo** (arrival)

---

# ✍️ Quick check

What is correct?

1. Vi er \_\_\_ i Oslo
2. Vi gikk \_\_\_ til døren
3. Vi er \_\_\_ nå

---

# ✅ Answers

1. fremme
2. frem
3. fremme
