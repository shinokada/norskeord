**“fremme”** is very common but not intuitive at first.

---

# 🧭 Meaning of *fremme*

**“fremme” = arrived / reached the destination**

---

# ✅ Your sentence:

* **Vi er fremme i Oslo**
  👉 *We have arrived in Oslo*
  👉 *We are now in Oslo (after traveling)*

---

# 🧠 Important: it’s NOT just “be in”

Compare:

### A.

* Vi er **i Oslo**
  → We are in Oslo (just location)

### B.

* Vi er **fremme i Oslo**
  → We arrived in Oslo (focus on arrival/result)

---

# 🚗 Think of it like this:

You travel → then you say:

* **Nå er vi fremme!**
  = We’ve arrived!

---

# 📍 More examples

* Vi er fremme **på hotellet**
* Toget er fremme **på stasjonen**
* Endelig er vi fremme!

---

# ⚠️ Common confusion

### ❌ Vi er fremme Oslo

👉 missing preposition

### ✅ Vi er fremme **i Oslo**

---

# 🔥 Compare with similar expressions

### 1. **komme fram**

* Vi **kom fram** til Oslo
  = We arrived (action)

### 2. **være fremme**

* Vi **er fremme** i Oslo
  = We have arrived (result/state)

---

# 🧠 Simple rule

* **komme fram** → movement
* **være fremme** → result (already there)

---

# ✍️ Quick practice

What sounds natural?

1. Vi er fremme ___ Oslo
2. Vi kom fram ___ Oslo
3. Endelig er vi ___

---

# ✅ Answers

1. i
2. til
3. fremme
