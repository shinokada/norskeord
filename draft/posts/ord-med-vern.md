### A useful pattern

**[what is being protected] + vern**

For example:

> **miljø + vern → miljøvern**
> **person + vern → personvern**
> **forbruker + vern → forbrukervern**
> **natur + vern → naturvern**
> **barn + vern → barnevern**

There is also an important distinction between **vern** and **beskyttelse**. In compounds, Norwegian very often prefers **-vern**:

* **miljøvern** rather than *miljøbeskyttelse*
* **personvern** rather than *personbeskyttelse*
* **barnevern** rather than *barnebeskyttelse*

But **vern** is not always interchangeable with **beskyttelse**; *vern* often has a stronger sense of **organized, legal, institutional, or systematic protection**.
