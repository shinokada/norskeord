# 🧭 Meaning

👉 **I need to get hold of her**
👉 **I need to reach her / contact her**
👉 **I need to find a way to contact her**

---

# 🧠 Word by word

* **jeg** = I
* **må** = must / need to
* **få tak i** = get hold of / manage to reach
* **henne** = her

---

# 🔑 Key idea: “få tak i”

This is a very common Norwegian expression.

👉 **få tak i = succeed in reaching/finding someone or something**

It is used when there is some difficulty or uncertainty.

---

# 📞 Typical situations

### 1. You can’t reach someone

* Jeg må få tak i henne
  → I need to reach her

* Jeg får ikke tak i henne
  → I can’t reach her

---

### 2. Finding something

* Jeg må få tak i en ny telefon
  → I need to get a new phone

* Klarer du å få tak i billettene?
  → Can you get hold of the tickets?

---

# ⚖️ Important comparison

| Expression           | Meaning         | Tone             |
| -------------------- | --------------- | ---------------- |
| kontakte henne       | contact her     | formal/neutral   |
| ringe henne          | call her        | specific method  |
| få tak i henne       | get hold of her | natural, general |
| ta kontakt med henne | get in touch    | slightly formal  |

---

# 🔥 Why “få” is important here

👉 **få = manage to / succeed in getting**

So:

* **få tak i** = manage to reach/get something

---
