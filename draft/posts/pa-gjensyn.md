Is "Takk for i dag. På gjensyn." common expression in Norwegian?

Yes, both are very common and natural in Norwegian, especially together at the end of a meeting, lesson, visit, or conversation.

* **Takk for i dag.** = “Thanks for today.”
* **På gjensyn.** = “See you again.”

Together:

> **Takk for i dag. På gjensyn.**

sounds polite, warm, and slightly formal.

Typical situations:

* after a Norwegian class
* leaving someone’s home
* after a meeting
* at a doctor, hairdresser, club activity, etc.

There are also more casual alternatives:

* **Takk for i dag, vi sees!** (“Thanks for today, see you!”)
* **Ha det!**
* **Snakkes!** (“Talk later!”)
* **Vi ses senere.**

A small nuance:

* **På gjensyn** is a bit more formal/old-fashioned than **vi sees** or **snakkes**, but it is still perfectly normal and polite. Older people and customer-service situations use it quite often.

Example:

> – Takk for i dag!
> – Takk selv. På gjensyn!

You may also hear:

> **Takk for i kveld** (“Thanks for this evening”)
> when leaving an evening gathering or dinner party.
