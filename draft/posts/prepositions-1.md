You’re right to focus on this—**prepositions are one of the hardest parts of Norwegian**, even at B2+. There’s logic, but also a lot of memorization.

---

# 🧭 1. Core idea (very important)

Think like this:

- **i** → inside / in
- **på** → on / at (surface, places, institutions)
- **ved** → by / near / using
- **med** → with
- **for** → for (purpose, duration, benefit)

---

# 📍 2. Most important prepositions explained

## **i** = inside / in

Used for:

- rooms, countries, cities
- time periods

### Examples:

- Jeg bor **i Oslo**
- Hun er **i huset**
- **I går**, **i fjor**, **i sommer**

---

## **på** = on / at (very common!)

Used for:

- surfaces
- many locations (schools, work, events)

### Examples:

- Boken ligger **på bordet**
- Jeg er **på jobb**
- Hun studerer **på universitetet**
- Vi er **på fest**

👉 “på” is often **just something you must learn**

---

## **ved** = by / near / using

Three main meanings:

### 1. Near a place

- Han sitter **ved bordet** (by the table)

### 2. Using something

- Hun reiste til Danmark **ved hjelp av ferje** ❌ (unnatural)
- Better: **med ferje** ✅

👉 “ved” is more formal for “using”

### 3. Fixed expressions

- **ved siden av** = next to
- **ved slutten av dagen**

---

## **med** = with

Used for:

- together
- tools / transport

### Examples:

- Jeg snakker **med henne**
- Jeg skriver **med penn**
- Jeg reiser **med tog / med ferje**

👉 This is why your sentence should be:

- **med ferje** ✅ (not _ved ferje_)

---

## **for** = for (reason, duration, benefit)

### Uses:

### 1. Duration

- Jeg bodde der **i tre år** (neutral)
- Jeg bodde der **for tre år siden** (time ago!)

👉 Important difference:

- **i tre år** = duration
- **for tre år siden** = ago

### 2. Reason

- Takk **for hjelpen**
- Jeg gjør det **for deg**

### 3. Too / excessive

- Det er **for dyrt**

---

# ⚠️ 3. Common mistakes (important)

### ❌ Jeg reiste ved buss

✅ Jeg reiste **med buss**

---

### ❌ Jeg er i jobb

✅ Jeg er **på jobb**

---

### ❌ Jeg bor på Oslo

✅ Jeg bor **i Oslo**

---

### ❌ Jeg ventet i lenge

✅ Jeg ventet **lenge**
