Because **hvor flink** means **“how good/skilled”**, while **hvordan flink** would ask about **the way/manner** in which someone is good—which is not what you mean here.

### Compare

**Han snakker om hvor flink han er.**
= He talks about **how good/skilled he is**.

Here, **hvor + adjective** expresses the **degree** of a quality:

* hvor flink = how good/skilled
* hvor vanskelig = how difficult
* hvor dyr = how expensive
* hvor viktig = how important
* hvor gammel = how old

For example:

> Jeg vet ikke **hvor vanskelig** det er.
> I don't know **how difficult** it is.

> Hun fortalte meg **hvor dyr** bilen var.
> She told me **how expensive** the car was.

### What does *hvordan* mean?

**Hvordan** means **“how / in what way”** and normally asks about a **method, manner, or process**:

> **Hvordan** gjorde han det?
> How did he do it?

> **Hvordan** snakker han med elevene?
> How does he talk to the students?

You could therefore think of the distinction as:

| Norwegian            | Meaning                      |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| **hvor + adjective** | how **[adjective]** / degree |
| **hvordan + verb**   | how / in what way            |

So:

> Han snakker om **hvor flink** han er.
> = He talks about **how good he is**. ✅

But:

> Han snakker om **hvordan** han er flink. ❌/unnatural

The second sentence would mean something like **“He talks about how he is good/skilled”**, as if you were going to explain *the way in which he demonstrates his skill*. It doesn't mean “how good he is.”

### A useful shortcut

When English **“how”** is followed by an **adjective**, Norwegian normally uses **hvor**:

* how good → **hvor flink**
* how big → **hvor stor**
* how fast → **hvor rask**
* how interesting → **hvor interessant**
* how difficult → **hvor vanskelig**

When English **“how”** is asking about **how something is done**, use **hvordan**:

* How did you do it? → **Hvordan gjorde du det?**
* How does it work? → **Hvordan fungerer det?**
* How do you make it? → **Hvordan lager du det?**

So in your sentence, **hvor** is essentially equivalent to **“how + adjective”** in English.
