hvordan kan det ha seg? is very **Norwegian and very useful** at B1/B2.

---

# 🧭 Meaning of **“hvordan kan det ha seg?”**

👉 It means:

* **How is that possible?**
* **How can that be?**
* **How did that happen?**

---

# 🧠 Literal breakdown (why it’s confusing)

* **hvordan** = how
* **kan** = can
* **det** = it
* **ha seg** = (idiomatic → “be the case / happen”)

👉 You **cannot translate “ha seg” directly**.
It’s an **expression**, not literal Norwegian.

---

# 🔥 Think of it like:

> “How can it be like that?”

---

# 📍 Examples

### 1.

* Han besto ikke eksamen.
* **Hvordan kan det ha seg?**
  👉 How is that possible?

---

### 2.

* Hun var ikke på jobb i dag.
* **Hvordan kan det ha seg?**

---

### 3.

* Det er mye billigere her.
* **Hvordan kan det ha seg?**

---

# ⚠️ Important: tone

This expression shows:

* surprise
* curiosity
* sometimes mild confusion

👉 Not aggressive, just wondering

---

# 🔁 Variations

### Slightly simpler:

* **Hvordan er det mulig?**
  = How is that possible?

### More direct:

* **Hva skjedde?**
  = What happened?

---

# 🧠 When to use it

Use it when:

* something is unexpected
* you want an explanation

---

# ✍️ Mini practice

Respond naturally:

* Han snakker flytende norsk etter bare 3 måneder.
  👉 Hva sier du?

✅ Possible answer:

* **Hvordan kan det ha seg?**

---

# 🧠 Final tip

This is a **very natural spoken phrase**, and using it will make your Norwegian sound much more fluent.
