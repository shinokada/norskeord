Both **galt** and **feil** can mean **“wrong”**, but they are used differently.

### 1. **feil** = wrong / incorrect / an error

**Feil** is the more general word for something being incorrect.

* **Det er feil.** = That's wrong.
* **Svaret er feil.** = The answer is wrong.
* **Jeg skrev feil.** = I wrote it incorrectly / I made a mistake.
* **Det var en feil.** = It was a mistake/error.
* **Du har feil.** = You are wrong.

It can also be a **noun**:

> **en feil** = a mistake/error/fault

For example:

> Jeg gjorde **en feil**.
> I made a mistake.

---

### 2. **galt** = wrong / bad / not right

**Galt** is the neuter form of **gal** and is commonly used when something is **not right, not correct, or going wrong**.

* **Hva er galt?** = What's wrong?
* **Noe er galt.** = Something is wrong.
* **Det gikk galt.** = It went wrong.
* **Jeg gjorde noe galt.** = I did something wrong.
* **Er det noe galt med bilen?** = Is something wrong with the car?

Here, **galt** is an adjective/adverbial form of **gal**:

> gal → galt
> wrong/crazy → wrong

---

### A very useful comparison

Compare:

> **Svaret er feil.**
> The answer is incorrect.

vs.

> **Noe er galt med svaret.**
> Something is wrong with the answer.

The first focuses on **incorrectness**. The second focuses on **something being wrong with it**.

Another important pair:

> **Jeg gjorde en feil.**
> I made a mistake.

> **Jeg gjorde noe galt.**
> I did something wrong.

The first says you made **an error**. The second says you **did something wrong**, often with a stronger sense that the action itself was inappropriate or incorrect.

### Common expressions

| Norwegian                      | English                               |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------- |
| **Det er feil.**               | That's wrong/incorrect.               |
| **Hva er galt?**               | What's wrong?                         |
| **Noe er galt.**               | Something is wrong.                   |
| **Det gikk galt.**             | It went wrong.                        |
| **Jeg gjorde en feil.**        | I made a mistake.                     |
| **Jeg gjorde noe galt.**       | I did something wrong.                |
| **Du har feil.**               | You're wrong.                         |
| **Det er noe galt med bilen.** | There's something wrong with the car. |
| **Det er feil på bilen.**      | There's a fault/problem with the car. |

One subtle point: **feil** is also used where English says **“wrong”**, so you shouldn't think of *feil* as only “incorrect” and *galt* as only “bad.” There is considerable overlap.

A good rule of thumb is:

**feil → incorrect / mistake / fault**
**galt → something is wrong / something went wrong / did something wrong**
