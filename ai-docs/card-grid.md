# /blog page

## The problem

You currently have 10 published posts and 50+ drafts on their way. The current /blog page is a flat list grouped by CEFR level with no way to filter, search, or discover content. Once you have 50–80 posts live, it becomes a long, hard-to-scroll wall.

## Solution

Card grid layout with pagination or "load more" (medium effort)
Replace the single flat list with a 2-column card grid (on desktop) and add either pagination or a "load more" button. This gives the page visual breathing room and keeps the initial render short.
