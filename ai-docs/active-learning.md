Study after study shows that students understand and remember more when they read printed material rather than content on screens. Similarly, people learn better when they write notes by hand instead of simply typing them.

Technology alone does not create learning. Real learning happens when you actively engage with the material. In fact, educational technology often has little benefit—or can even be distracting—if it is used passively.

To get the most out of Norskeord, try to learn actively. Write new words and expressions on paper, create your own example sentences, listen and repeat aloud, and review vocabulary regularly. Norskeord can help you discover and understand Norwegian words and expressions, but lasting learning comes from your own active practice.
