### Free categories is probably too few — your instinct is right

My revised suggestion: **make all categories free, gate FSRS and the email service behind Plus**. Reasons:

- Users who study all A1 categories and want proper scheduling are *exactly* the motivated users who will pay
- You compete with Anki (free, powerful) and Duolingo (free, gamified) — a content gate at 3 categories will lose people before they're hooked
- The email service + FSRS is a genuinely differentiated Plus offer that free tools don't provide

This is a meaningful strategic shift worth discussing before updating the docs.


### 3. Flashcard + email alone may not be enough to convert — you need the scheduling hook

The honest answer: a flashcard app with audio is a *good free tool*, but it doesn't have a strong enough reason to pay on its own. The email service is novel but it's an add-on, not a core reason to upgrade.

What actually converts in language apps is **progress that feels real and personal**. FSRS does this — "you have 12 cards due today, 3 are overdue, you know 67% of B1" is sticky in a way that a category list isn't. That's your conversion engine.

So the Plus pitch should be: **FSRS scheduling + progress tracking + email service**, not just email. The stats page showing your honest CEFR estimate is also part of this — show it to free users but make it more detailed for Plus (per-card history, forecast, etc.).
