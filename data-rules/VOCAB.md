# Vocab format for substantiv/verb/adjektiv

The key question is: what's the card's job? If it's a flashcard for recognition and recall (not a grammar drill), then the form shown on the card shapes what the learner memorises.
My recommendation would be:

1. Substantiv: hus (et) — show the dictionary form with gender in parentheses. This is the most useful single piece of information a learner needs beyond the word itself: knowing it's et vs en is essential for all subsequent grammar. I'd avoid hus(et) or hus(-et) — the parenthetical suffix notation is dictionary shorthand that looks odd on a flashcard and is less readable, especially on mobile.
2. Verb: å få — the infinitive with å prefix. It's how Norwegians naturally cite a verb ("hva betyr å få?"), it's unambiguous as a verb, and it avoids the conjugation table problem. få/fikk/fått is useful but that's grammar drill territory, not flashcard territory — and your grammar feature can handle that.
3. Adjektiv: dictionary form — adjectives inflect predictably enough (most of the time) that showing glad and letting the grammar system handle glad/glatt/glade is the right split.

So: hus (et), å få, glad. Clean, readable, consistently useful without being a dictionary.
